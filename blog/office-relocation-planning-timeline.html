Office relocation: a 12-week planning timeline

Office relocations succeed or fail in the planning. A well-planned 50-person office move can complete over a weekend with zero downtime; a poorly-planned one bleeds productivity for weeks. This 12-week timeline covers the project plan we use with Staffordshire commercial customers — the activities that need to happen, when, and who's responsible.

Section 1

Week 12: project initiation

Confirm move date. The single most important decision. Lock in lease end at the old premises and lease start at the new premises so they overlap by at least 1-2 weeks. Inform the project team.

Assign project owner. One person responsible end-to-end — usually office manager, ops director or HR. Without single ownership, office moves fragment across multiple stakeholders and lose coherence.

Book the removal company. 12 weeks ahead is the sweet spot for commercial moves. Get 2-3 fixed-price quotes; the cheapest is rarely the best — look for in-house crews (not sub-contractors), commercial cover, and experience with your scale of move.

Survey both properties. Loading bays, lift dimensions, parking restrictions, access hours, building-management approvals — all need confirming at both ends well in advance.

Section 2

Week 10-11: IT and infrastructure

IT decommission plan. Server racks, desktop PCs, monitors, peripherals, cabling. Whose responsibility is each — internal IT, outsourced IT provider, or removal company?

Connectivity at new premises. Confirm internet provider, install lead time, line-of-sight to backup connectivity. Typical lead time for new business fibre: 4-8 weeks. Order now.

Phone system. VoIP systems move easily; legacy PBX systems may need cabling work at the new premises. Confirm with your provider.

Building security. Door codes, alarm systems, key handover at both ends. Plan for the locksmith if needed.

Section 3

Week 8-9: staff communications and logistics

All-hands announcement. Brief all staff on the date, the new address, parking arrangements, dress code (if changed), and what staff need to do personally (pack their desk, clear personal storage, take any sensitive items home).

Travel impact assessment. Survey staff for the impact of the move on their commute. Some staff may need adjusted contracts (working hours, hybrid arrangements) if the new location significantly worsens their commute.

Furniture inventory. Walk the existing office with the removal company surveyor. Decide what moves to the new premises, what gets disposed of, what gets sold or donated. Old office furniture often has zero resale value; budget for disposal cost rather than expecting income.

New furniture orders. If you're upgrading furniture for the new premises, lead times can run 4-8 weeks. Order now so delivery aligns with move-in.

Section 4

Week 6-7: pre-move planning detail

Move-day timetable. Hour-by-hour plan for the move weekend. Start time, finish time, IT decommission window, furniture-move window, IT recommission window, staff move-in time Monday.

Crate hire delivery. Plastic crates are dramatically better than cardboard for office moves — more secure for IT equipment, easier to label by department, reusable. Crate-hire delivery 1-2 weeks before the move; collection 1-2 weeks after.

Sensitive document handling. Confirm the chain-of-custody for any sensitive documents (HR files, financial records, client files). Some documents may need couriered transport rather than going on the removal lorry.

Insurance check. Confirm commercial Goods in Transit cover with your removal company. £10m Public Liability minimum at the new premises; certificates of cover for building management. Business interruption insurance — review whether your existing policy covers move-related downtime.

Section 5

Week 4-5: utility and address changes

Utility transfers. Gas, electricity, water at both ends. Final meter readings on move day; new accounts active from move-in day. Get the supplier names lined up now.

Address update list. Bank, HMRC, Companies House, insurance providers, professional indemnity insurers, regulatory bodies, customers, suppliers, payroll, pension scheme, business credit cards. Build the list now; trigger the updates the week before the move.

Post redirect. Royal Mail business redirect: 3-12 months recommended for businesses. Apply 2-3 weeks before move day.

Signage at the new premises. Branding, reception signage, room signs. Order now if you don't have it already.

Section 6

Week 2-3: packing and final prep

Department-by-department packing. Crates delivered. Each department packs its own materials, with our crew supporting any heavy or specialist items. Label every crate with department + destination room.

IT decommission rehearsal. Run through the IT-down process with your IT provider. Identify any unexpected dependencies — servers other systems rely on, services that can't be down longer than expected.

Staff personal-item packing. Staff pack their own desks the day before the move. Personal items go home with the staff member (not into the lorry) to avoid loss confusion.

Section 7

Move weekend: the move itself

Friday evening: staff leave by agreed time, IT begins decommission, removal crew arrives, loading starts. Typical 50-person office: 6-10 hours to fully load.

Saturday: drive to new premises, unload, place furniture per the floor plan, reassemble desks, IT recommissions the network and key systems.

Sunday: finishing touches — final IT recommissioning, signage hanging, deep clean. Senior staff visit to confirm everything is in place for Monday.

Monday morning: staff arrive at the new premises. IT support on-site through the morning to handle individual workstation issues. Project lead floats to address whatever comes up.

Section 8

Week 1 post-move: stabilisation

IT issue logging. Track every workstation issue raised in the first week. Most resolve quickly; a handful uncover unexpected configuration issues that need wider attention.

Staff feedback round. Quick all-hands check-in at end of week 1. What's working, what isn't, what needs addressing.

Crate collection. Empty crates go back to the removal company 1-2 weeks after the move.

Old premises clear-down. Final clean, key handover to landlord, schedule inspection for the deposit return.

FAQ

Common questions

How far in advance should I book an office relocation?

12 weeks ahead for a 30+ desk office; 6-8 weeks for smaller. Below 4 weeks notice you may struggle to find good removers and IT support.

Can the office move happen over a weekend with no downtime?

Yes for most 5-50 desk offices. Friday evening load, Saturday delivery and reassembly, Sunday final IT, Monday operational. This is our standard commercial pattern.

Who's responsible for the IT decommission and recommission?

Usually your internal IT team or external IT provider — not the removal company. We move the equipment; they configure it. Coordinate the handover at survey.

Do you provide plastic crates for office moves?

Yes — plastic crates are dramatically better than cardboard for office contents. Delivered 1-2 weeks ahead, collected 1-2 weeks after.

What's the biggest cause of office move delays?

Connectivity at the new premises. New business fibre takes 4-8 weeks to install — order it at week 10-12, not week 2-3.
